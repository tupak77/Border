Pre-send QA

The checklist every send goes through.

Before anything reaches the marketing leads for approval. It takes a few minutes, and it's the reason nobody else has to find the mistakes.

Audience

  • Segment logic matches the brief, condition by condition
  • Count checked against last similar send
  • Spot-check 10 profiles by hand
  • Exclusions and suppressions applied
  • Frequency cap: not mailed too recently

Content

  • Personalisation fallbacks on every tag
  • Every link clicked, UTMs correct
  • Discount codes tested at checkout
  • Preview text, subject, sender name
  • Desktop, mobile and dark mode render

Timing & context

  • Send time in the right time zone
  • Smart send / quiet hours as intended
  • No clash with flows or other campaigns
  • Sensitivity check for the segment
  • Approval logged in Slack

Kept as a template in the team's docs, so the process doesn't depend on my memory and anyone can pick it up.

Sensitive sends

When a correct email is still the wrong email.

The job post asks for judgment about how a message lands with a specific segment. Three examples of what I'd flag before it goes out.

Price

Discount to people who just paid full price

RiskA 30% off email the day after someone bought at full price feels like a penalty for buying.

FlagExclude recent full-price buyers, or ask the leads if they want a separate note for them.

Timing

A cheerful campaign on a hard day

RiskA playful send scheduled during a major news event or a sensitive date for part of the audience.

FlagRaise it to the leads with a suggested new time. Their call, made with the facts.

Context

A promo to someone with an open complaint

RiskA "we miss you" offer to a customer waiting on a refund or support reply.

FlagSuggest excluding profiles with open tickets, if the data is available in Klaviyo.
