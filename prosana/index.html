Offers

Save the subscriber, not the discount.

Discounts are the last rung, not the first. A sketch of how I'd structure save offers and gift ladders for a supplement subscription.

Cancel flow · save offer by reason

Answer the real reason first

Reason"I have too much product"
OfferSkip or delay the next order. No discount needed.
Reason"It's too expensive"
OfferSwap to a smaller size or longer interval, then a one-time discount.
Reason"Not seeing results yet"
OfferEducation on timing and how to take it, plus one free month to give it a fair try.
Reason"Taking a break"
OfferPause 30 to 90 days, with a reminder before it restarts.

Gift ladder · by rebill

Reward staying, not leaving

Rebill 2Free accessory (shaker, pill case)
Rebill 3Sample of a new product or flavour
Rebill 4Early access to launches
Rebill 6Full-size gift + referral ask

Announced before rebill 1, so the reason to stay is visible exactly when the risk is highest. Every rung gets a holdout to prove it pays for itself.

Rules I'd hold every test to

No result without a decision

  • Hypothesis written first, with the metric that decides it.
  • Sized before launch. If an account can't reach the sample, test a bigger change or run it longer.
  • Built on the metrics that matter: zero-day churn, M1/M2 retention, rebills and cohort LTV.
  • Data validated before the client sees it.
  • Conclusion recorded in a shared log, so the next pod doesn't run it again.
