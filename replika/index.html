First 90 days

Building it from the ground up.

Written from the outside, so the order will change once I see the data. Email first, as the role says.

Days 1–30

Foundations

  • Audit consent, unsubscribes and preferences by region before sending anything.
  • Map the base: free users, active subscribers, lapsed subscribers, long-dormant sign-ups.
  • Check events and billing data so flows trigger on what really happened.
  • Set up domains, authentication and a holdout for every programme.

Days 31–60

Core flows

  • Onboarding that helps people get to a good first week.
  • Free-to-paid at moments of real value, not on a timer.
  • Renewal and failed-payment flows: the fastest revenue win.
  • One weekly dashboard on retention and revenue.

Days 61–90

Win-back and more channels

  • Bring back lapsed subscribers first, then the dormant list in small, warmed batches.
  • Add push and in-app where they help the email journeys.
  • Launch comms for new features.
  • Keep, change or drop each test based on incremental results.

Each flow gets a goal, a holdout and a date to decide whether to keep it.
