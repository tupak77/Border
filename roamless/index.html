Braze migration

How I'd help with the move.

Migrations go wrong in the details. This is the order I'd follow, with your team setting the priorities.

STEP 01

Inventory

  • List every live campaign, template and segment.
  • Keep, merge or retire each one.
  • Agree naming conventions first.

STEP 02

Data & segments

  • Check events and attributes arrive correctly.
  • Rebuild segments by lifecycle stage and behaviour.
  • Set up control groups.

STEP 03

Templates & journeys

  • Reusable HTML email templates.
  • Rebuild onboarding and first-purchase journeys first.
  • Translations and personalisation checked.

STEP 04

QA & go-live

  • Test sends for every variant.
  • Compare results with the old setup.
  • Document everything for the team.
