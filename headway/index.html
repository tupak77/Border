Provider journey

Four moments worth a message.

A first sketch from the outside, using only what's public. The real map comes from Headway's data and from Product and Customer Success.

Activation

First session, first payment

The moment the admin promise becomes real. Make it visible and remove the next blocker.

  • credentialed, no session yet
  • first claim paid

metric time to first session

Adoption

The second tool

Providers who use more than billing get more value. Introduce one tool at the moment it saves time.

  • steady caseload
  • never opened a feature

metric feature adoption · 30d

Engagement

A practice that runs itself

Monthly recaps that show the time and admin they didn't have to do. Useful, not promotional.

  • active, multi-feature
  • new capability shipped

metric monthly active providers

Retention

Early signs of drifting

Fewer sessions or logins can mean many things. Ask first, offer help second, never assume.

  • activity drop vs own baseline
  • unresolved support issue

metric provider retention · 90d

Each moment gets one owner, one trigger definition agreed with Data, and a holdout so we know it's the programme and not the season.

Test-and-learn

Every campaign makes the next one smarter.

Three first experiments, written the way I'd write them before launch: hypothesis, success metric, and what we'd do with the answer.

TEST 01 · channel

In-product vs email for feature adoption

Hypothesis
Providers active in the app adopt a feature more from an in-product prompt than from email.
Success
Feature used within 14 days, vs holdout.
Then
Sets the default channel for every adoption programme.
TEST 02 · timing

Behaviour trigger vs calendar day

Hypothesis
"After 5 sessions" beats "day 21" for introducing a time-saving tool.
Success
Adoption at 30 days, with no rise in unsubscribes.
Then
Moves the programme from time-based to behaviour-based triggers.
TEST 03 · message

Time saved vs feature list

Hypothesis
"Get back an hour a week" outperforms describing what the feature does.
Success
Click-to-adoption rate.
Then
Becomes the message pattern, shared with Product Marketing.

Results go into one shared log, so a learning from one programme is reused in the next instead of rediscovered.

Six-month roadmap

Learn, build, then compound.

Written from the outside, so the order will change once I'm inside and working with the first lifecycle hire.

MONTHS 1–2

Understand

  • Learn Iterable and Intercom setups and every live programme.
  • Listen to Customer Success calls; talk to providers.
  • Map the provider journey and its key moments with Product.
  • Agree metrics and holdout rules with Data.

MONTHS 3–4

Build

  • Launch the first adoption programme, behaviour-triggered.
  • Run the channel and timing tests.
  • Align in-product and email so they don't overlap.
  • Set up lifecycle reporting by stage.

MONTHS 5–6

Compound

  • Add engagement and early-retention programmes.
  • Roll test learnings into every journey.
  • Read results against holdouts; keep, change or stop.
  • Next six-month roadmap, based on what moved behaviour.

Every programme gets a goal, a holdout and a date to decide whether to keep it.

Copy

Written for people with a full caseload.

My own samples, based on public information. Not real Headway messages; product details would be checked with Product.

EmailActivation

trigger: first claim paid

Your first payment is on its way

Hi Dr. Rivera, your first session through Headway has been paid. No claim forms, no follow-up calls.

That's how it should work every time. If anything ever looks off, our team is one message away.

Why: celebrates the moment the promise becomes real. No client details, no upsell.

In-productAdoption

trigger: sessions ≥ 5 · feature not used

You've seen 5 clients this month. Want an hour back?

Providers with a steady caseload use this to spend less time on admin each week. It takes two minutes to set up.

Why: shows up when the time saving is real for them, and leads with the outcome, not the feature.

EmailRetention

trigger: activity below own baseline · 3 weeks

Anything we can make easier?

Hi Sam, we noticed things have been quieter on your account lately. That can mean a lot of things, including a well-earned break.

If something on our side is getting in the way, reply and a real person will help.

Why: asks before assuming, makes a break feel normal, and offers a person, not a promo.
