Driver journey

Five stages, one goal: come back.

A first sketch from the outside. The real journey comes from EVgo's data, Product and driver research.

01

Sign-up

Get to a first charge fast: nearest station, how to start, what it costs.

trigger: account, no charge in 7d

kpi first charge rate

02

First charge

Make it feel easy, save the station, set expectations for next time.

trigger: session complete

kpi 2nd charge in 30d

03

Habit

Show plug-and-charge when they've charged a few times and it saves real friction.

trigger: 3 sessions, no Autocharge+

kpi Autocharge+ adoption

04

Subscribe

Offer a plan when their own charging pattern shows it pays off.

trigger: monthly spend above break-even

kpi subscription conversion

05

Loyal

Keep subscribers seeing the value; catch drift before they lapse.

trigger: drop vs own baseline

kpi retention · churn

Every stage gets one trigger defined with Data, one success metric and a holdout, so we know the programme moved drivers, not the season.

Compliance

Fast, and safe to send.

SMS · TCPA

Consent first

  • Express written consent recorded
  • STOP honoured instantly
  • Quiet hours by time zone

Email · CAN-SPAM

Clear and honest

  • Accurate sender and subject
  • Working unsubscribe, every send
  • Opt-outs processed quickly

Lists · Braze

Clean by default

  • Suppression lists applied to every campaign
  • Frequency caps across channels
  • Edge cases escalated, not guessed
