Copy

A few drafts for a DTC brand.

Written for a made-up coffee brand to show tone and structure.

EmailReplenishment · day 24

Running low? Your beans are probably on their last week.

Reorder in one click, same grind as last time.

Hi Maya, most people finish a 250g bag in about 4 weeks. Reorder now and it arrives before you run out.

Want to try something new instead? Here's what people who liked yours buy next.

Why: timed from real reorder data, removes effort, and offers discovery for those ready for a change.

SMSCart abandon · 1h

Hey Maya, your Ethiopia roast is still in your cart. It's our best-seller this month, and stock is low. Finish your order: [link]

Why: short, specific to the product, one honest reason to act now, one link. No discount on the first touch.

EmailPost-purchase · referral

Glad you loved it. Know someone who would too?

Thanks for the 5-star review, Maya. Give a friend 15% off their first bag, and you'll get a free bag when they order.

Why: sent only after a positive review, the moment someone is most likely to recommend.
