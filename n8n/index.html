What makes a kit get used

Six rules, from lifecycle.

1

One first step, under 10 minutes

If the first step takes an afternoon, it never happens. Make the first win small and fast.

2

Works as it is

Every template should be usable without editing. Customising is optional, not required.

3

Nudge at the stall point

Follow up when people usually stop, with help, not a reminder that they're behind.

4

Localise the 20% that matters

Language, examples and dates change. Structure and design stay the same everywhere.

5

Show what others got

"12 partners ran this last month, 40 meetings booked" sells a kit better than any description.

6

Measure execution, then stop or scale

Each kit gets a run-rate target and a date. Below it, change it or retire it.
