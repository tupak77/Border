Economy

Sources, sinks, and who pays for the free stuff.

How I'd look at the economy from day one. A sketch from the outside, not a description of Patrianna's games.

Made-up sources and sinks. The CRM bonus is the only source I'd treat as a line of cost, with a price per segment.

Rule 01 · Price before you give

Every bonus gets an expected return by segment before it ships. If I can't forecast it in NGR, it doesn't go out.

Rule 02 · Missions beat discounts

For players who aren't price-sensitive, give them something to do rather than something cheaper. It keeps the economy healthy and the margin intact.

Rule 03 · Liquidity is retention

An empty contest is a bad session. Reactivation timed to fill the contests that need players helps both sides of the economy.

Rule 04 · Liability on the dashboard

Outstanding bonus balance tracked next to NGR, every week, so nobody gets surprised at month end.

How I work

Hypothesis in, decision out.

Campaign read-out · illustration

Win-back: mission chain vs deposit bonus

Hypothesis
Lapsed mid-value players come back as often for a mission chain as for a bonus, at lower cost.
Design
3 arms + 10% holdout, sample size fixed before launch, 30-day window.
Read-out
Measured on incremental NGR minus bonus cost, not reactivation rate alone.
Decision
Keep, change or stop, and the learning goes into the next brief.
