What I'd want to build here

Two loops, one goal.

HubFit has two kinds of retention: coaches staying with HubFit, and clients staying with their coach. They feed each other. Written from the outside, as a starting point for a conversation.

Loop 1 · coaches

Coaches who stick

Lifecycle for HubFit's own customers.

  1. Activation: get a new coach to their first client invited and first programme delivered, fast.
  2. Adoption: introduce the next feature when it saves them time, like check-ins or nutrition, not on a calendar.
  3. Upgrade: show a coach the plan they need when their client list grows.
  4. Retention: spot coaches who stop logging in and help before they cancel.

measure time to first client · trial-to-paid · coach churn

Loop 2 · coaches' clients

Clients who show up

Helping coaches keep their own clients.

  1. Ready-made journeys coaches switch on: welcome, first week, missed workouts, milestones.
  2. At-risk alerts when a client goes quiet, with a check-in written in the coach's voice.
  3. Progress moments: celebrate personal bests and streaks automatically.
  4. Renewal recaps: show clients their progress before they pay again.

measure client retention per coach · coach revenue

First 90 days

Listen, ship, measure.

Written from the outside, so the order will change once I'm inside.

DAYS 1–30

Listen

  • Talk to coaches who stayed and coaches who left.
  • Map the coach journey from sign-up to paid.
  • Audit every live email, push and in-app message.
  • Agree the key metrics and holdout rules.

DAYS 31–60

Ship

  • Rebuild coach onboarding around the first client.
  • Launch a feature-adoption journey triggered by behaviour.
  • First A/B tests on onboarding and trial conversion.

DAYS 61–90

Measure

  • Read results against holdouts; keep, change or stop.
  • At-risk coach programme.
  • Proposal for client-retention tools for coaches, with Product.

Every programme gets a goal, a holdout and a date to decide whether to keep it.
