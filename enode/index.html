AI workflows

A team of one that works like five.

Four workflows I'd build in the first months. Each one has a person checking the output before anything reaches a customer.

01

Account research

Tariff launches, job posts, earnings calls, regulatory filings and press, summarised into one brief per target account, refreshed weekly.

in account list
out brief + signals in HubSpot

02

Buying-group mapping

Who in product, tech, trading and finance is likely involved, what each one cares about, and which of them we already know.

in brief + CRM contacts
out map + gaps for the AE

03

Personalised outreach

First drafts of emails and LinkedIn messages per role, built from the brief and our positioning. I edit; the AE approves.

in brief + message house
out drafts ready to review

04

Pipeline reporting

A weekly read of HubSpot: which accounts moved, which touches came before, and where to put the next euro and hour.

in HubSpot deals + activity
out one-page weekly note

The point isn't more emails. It's knowing each account well enough that the few messages we send are worth a reply.
